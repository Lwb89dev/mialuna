Mialuna is a visual social network powered by Nostr.

Share photos, carousels, videos, Reels and Stories in an elegant, decentralized environment — no central platform, no ads, no tracking.

- Media-first: no text-only posts
- Decentralized: your keys, your relays, your media servers (Blossom)
- Sign in with Amber (NIP-55), a remote bunker (NIP-46) or browse as guest
- Stories expire after 24 hours (NIP-40)
- Open source (MIT)

Your moments, under the same moon.
